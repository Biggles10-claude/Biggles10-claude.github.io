WorldofAI posts almost every day about new AI models, coding agents, free model windows and credits. The 9 Oct boards caught some of the same news, but missed his "free this week" and "new feature in a tool you already use" items. What changed in the skill, and why

Before

  • Leads came only from web, X, Reddit, Hacker News and GitHub searches.
  • The rule "A tweet or listicle may lead you there. It is never the citation." didn't mention video.
  • Deals meant "a price cut, free tier, trial, or credit", with no instructions on where to look.
  • No query templates for free windows, stealth models or install pages.
  • No floor for creator feeds.

After

  • A new creator-channel lead lane: WorldofAI RSS is the lead source, plus AICodeKing, Matthew Berman, Sam Witteveen, Cole Medin, Prompt Engineering and IndyDevDan. Lead sites: the OpenRouter models API, OpenCode Zen docs, Product Hunt, Hugging Face and GitHub trending, AINews and AI Promo Now.
  • A video is a lead, never the citation. The citation is the repo, docs, model card or vendor page.
  • A free tools, launches and credits angle, plus new features in tools you already run and how-to-install. Install lines are quoted from the official page, never from the video.
  • Twelve query templates, plus a floor: WorldofAI and 3 other feeds and 2 sites opened, with every lead traced to a primary page or dropped.
  • Extra kill rules for leaks with no official page, the creator's own leaderboard used as the proof, affiliate-only offers, and one-shot demos used as proof of fit.

Why: the channel finds free windows and new-feature news a day or two before they reach the web searches the skill already runs. Those windows often close within a week, like MiniMax's 7 Oct end date and TinyFish's 16 Oct end date. Reading his feed early catches them in time. The gates then filter out the hype, which is most of what he posts.

Kept exactly as before: all three gates, the must-beat sentence, the kill list (new lines were only added), the drop rule for general LLMs, the 7-day install rule, the [OFFICIAL]/[COMMUNITY] deal tags, the five newsdesk lanes, and the plain-English voice. The patch only adds lines. Six existing lines were extended, and none were removed.
